About Me

Get to know me more

Hello world I'm an English Language Teaching student who want to make Teaching Business. I am a dedicated student in the Faculty of Education, majoring in English Teaching at Panyapiwat Institute of Management. I am a firm believer in the Work-based Education model, which emphasizes learning through practical experience. With a B2 CEFR proficiency level and a deep commitment to the teaching profession, I focus on integrating academic excellence with effective classroom management to inspire students through a sincere and professional approach.

My expertise lies in designing innovative instructional materials through Game-based Learning (GBL), with a specific focus on applying Morphology concepts, as demonstrated in my project, "The Forge." As a detail-oriented individual and a perfectionist, I constantly explore ways to leverage technology and modern methodologies to create a globally-minded and engaging classroom environment. My goal is to elevate the English learning experience, making it more dynamic and impactful for the next generation.
